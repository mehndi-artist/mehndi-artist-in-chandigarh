Mehndi Guidelines for the Deepest Stain

A beautiful design needs proper care to achieve that rich maroon or deep black stain. Follow these simple tips before and after our session to ensure your henna photographs beautifully:

  • Wash your hands and legs thoroughly before the application. Avoid applying heavy body lotions or oils as they create a barrier.
  • Wear comfortable clothing, preferably something with loose sleeves and pant legs.
  • Leave the organic henna paste on your skin for at least 6 to 8 hours. Keeping it on overnight is highly recommended.
  • Once removed, gently apply a mixture of lemon juice and sugar, followed by mustard or eucalyptus oil to seal the color.
  • Avoid direct contact with water for the first 12 hours after scraping off the paste.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my bridal mehndi?

We recommend booking at least 2 to 3 months in advance, particularly if your wedding falls during the busy winter season or auspicious dates.

Do you travel to locations outside Chandigarh and Panchkula?

Yes, we actively travel. We frequently provide services in Solan, Kasauli, Shimla, Jalandhar, Ludhiana, Amritsar, Gurudaspur, and are known as the best mehndi artist in Kalka and Pinjore areas.

Is your henna safe and chemical-free?

Absolutely. We prepare our henna paste at home using 100% natural henna powder, essential oils, lemon juice, and sugar. We never use chemical dyes or "fast-color" cones.

How long does the bridal application take?

A standard bridal application for hands (up to elbows) and feet (up to calves) takes roughly 4 to 6 hours, depending on the intricacy of the design.

Can you create custom designs from a reference picture?

Yes. If you have screenshots or specific patterns you love, show them to us. We will recreate them or blend different elements to create your perfect custom design.

Do you provide mehndi services for guests?

Yes, we have a team of skilled artists who accompany the lead artist to handle family members and guests during the Sangeet or Mehndi function.

How can I guarantee a dark stain?

Leaving the paste on for 8 hours, applying heat (like from cloves), using the lemon-sugar sealant, and avoiding water contact for 12 hours will yield the darkest results.

What is your pricing structure?

Our pricing depends on the design length, detail level, and travel distance. Please contact us on WhatsApp with your requirements for an exact, tailored quote.

Do I need to arrange anything for the artist?

All we need is a comfortable seating arrangement with good lighting, a small table for our supplies, and access to an electrical outlet if extra lighting is required.

How do I pay the advance deposit?

We accept deposits via Google Pay, Paytm, PhonePe, or direct bank transfer. The remaining balance is cleared on the day of the event.
